## How to read the results

Each question runs up to three searches. The score is the top score (cosine, higher is closer).

- **All**: All uploads. The rank is the position of the first chunk from an expected
  upload (`upload` or `alsoIn`) in the top 5.
- **One**: scoped to the question's `upload`.
- **Wrong**: scoped to the question's `wrongUpload` (answerable questions only). The
  right result is "not found".
- **At 0.62**: what the threshold does. For answerable questions: whether All and One
  are kept (at or above it), and whether the wrong-upload search is rejected (below it). For the
  others: rejected, or passed on to the LLM.
