## How to read the results

Each question runs up to four searches: All, One, Wrong, and Several (below) when it has a
`several` list. A score is the search's top score (rerank score (rerank-3, higher is more relevant)).

- **All**: the top score, searching All uploads.
- **Upload at** (after All): position of the first chunk from an expected upload (`upload`
  or `alsoIn`) in the top 10, whether or not it contains the answer; **miss** if none.
- **Answer at** (after All and after One): position of the first chunk that contains one of
  the question's `expect` phrases (copied from the sample), or **missing**. It checks that the
  passage with the answer came back whole. It can sit below "Upload at" when another chunk
  of the same upload ranks above it: the right upload, but not the passage with the answer.
- **One**: the top score, searching only the question's `upload`.
- **Wrong**: the top score, searching only the question's `wrongUpload` (answerable
  questions only). The right result is "not found".
- **At 0.58**: what the threshold does with the top score. At or above it, the
  question is **sent to the LLM**; below it, it's **stopped**: "not found" without calling the
  LLM. Answerable questions should be sent to the LLM in All and One, and stopped in Wrong;
  the others should be stopped. A wrong outcome is in bold.
